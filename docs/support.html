Kids Lock Shorts Support

Kids Lock Shorts blocks YouTube Shorts in Safari on iPhone and iPad. If you need help, try these steps:

  • Make sure the Kids Lock Shorts extension is turned on in Safari settings and allowed on YouTube.
  • Reload the YouTube page after changing the extension settings.

Contact support

Email xguo10@tpg.com.au and describe what happened. Including your device, operating system and Safari versions, along with steps to reproduce the issue, can help us investigate.
