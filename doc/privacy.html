Kids Lock Shorts Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Kids Lock Shorts is a Safari extension, with a companion app, for iPhone, iPad and Mac. It blocks YouTube Shorts in Safari. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: it collects nothing.

Data we collect

None. Kids Lock Shorts does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal information, browsing history, usage data or identifiers. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking. It also sends no crash reports of its own.

How the extension works

The extension runs only on pages at youtube.com and its subdomains, such as m.youtube.com. Inside your browser, it:

  • redirects pages whose address starts with /shorts/ to the YouTube home page,
  • stops clicks on links to Shorts, and
  • hides Shorts sections on YouTube pages.

All of this happens on your device. The extension makes no network requests of its own, and it doesn't read, record or send the content of the pages you visit. It requests no browser permissions beyond running on YouTube pages.

The companion app

The app only shows setup instructions and checks whether the extension is turned on. It collects no data and makes no network requests.

Third parties

YouTube is operated by Google. Its own privacy policy governs any data YouTube collects when you visit it. Kids Lock Shorts doesn't change that, and it is not affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube or Google.

Children

Kids Lock Shorts is a tool for parents and guardians, and it collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
